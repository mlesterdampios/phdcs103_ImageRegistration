# Third-party material

## Camera photograph

The camera photograph is by **Lav Varshney**, provided through `skimage.data.camera()` and identified by the provider as **CC0**. Resized, moved and recolored derivatives are researcher-created controlled conditions. The image appears in experiment inputs and in embedded demo/notebook illustrations.

Provider: <https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.data.html#skimage.data.camera>.

## ITK MRI teaching images

`experiments/data/brain_pd.png` and `brain_t1.png` are the public ITK proton-density and T1 MRI teaching images. They come from the pinned ITK source commit `50c5b33430e98b91b53fbaae2dd94f0edb2a639a`. The repository's **Apache License 2.0** text is retained in [ITK_LICENSE.txt](experiments/data/ITK_LICENSE.txt); exact source URLs and processing details appear in [PROVENANCE.md](experiments/data/PROVENANCE.md).

The supplied files do not establish patient metadata, physical pixel spacing, acquisition provenance or independent original anatomical truth. The researcher evaluates recovery of a deliberately added movement.
